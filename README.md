#AGROTECH

An intelligent agricultural assistant designed to help farmers and growers monitor crop health, identify plant diseases early, and optimize farm management.

## Key Features

* **Plant Disease Detection:** Scan or upload photos of leaves and crops to instantly identify diseases, nutrient deficiencies, and pest damage.
* **Actionable Treatment Guides:** Receive targeted organic and chemical remediation steps alongside preventive care advice.
* **Crop Health & Advisory:** Get practical insights on soil conditions, seasonal planting, and yield optimization.

## How It Works

1. Capture or upload a clear photo of the affected plant or crop.
2. Let the vision engine analyze symptoms and classify potential issues.
3. Review the diagnosis and follow the recommended treatment plan. (amongst others)
